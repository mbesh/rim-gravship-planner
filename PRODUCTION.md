# Production catalog

Checked against the [RimWorld Wiki production list](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Production), [Biotech buildings](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Biotech_(Buildings)), [Anomaly buildings](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Buildings)), and the linked item pages on 2026-11-25.

The Production palette contains all 26 base-game production buildings, two supporting facilities (tool cabinet and multi-analyzer), ten Biotech production/support machines, and three Anomaly production machines. Royalty, Ideology, and Odyssey recipes that use existing benches do not create duplicate bench types. Other DLC furniture and non-production buildings belong to their own categories.

All 41 items are available for planning regardless of the Vanilla Gravship Expanded chapter settings. DLC requirements are labels rather than ownership restrictions. There are no ship-wide component limits for these buildings; limits on the number of linked facilities per bench are not ship-wide limits.

Footprints are rectangles in the listed orientation; rotation swaps width and height. Gene assembler and subcore softscanner use 3 × 2 to match the available south/north artwork (the wiki lists the equivalent turned footprint, 2 × 3). The 1 × 4 hydroponics basin uses the original north/east images. Initial placement chooses a supplied original facing, and rotation uses other directional images when available, otherwise rotated artwork. Fixed spots, campfires, barrels, drills, multi-analyzers, and gene processors do not show rotation controls.

Use the usual placement, move, remove, Undo/Redo and browser save/load controls, including touch dragging and selected-component rotation. Missing substructure and out-of-field placements remain red. Work recipes, output rates, fuel use, electricity consumption, facility links/bonuses, interaction-cell access, pawn/mech requirements, terrain, containment, and growing conditions are not simulated. Refineries do not automatically refill ship fuel tanks, and production consumers do not change the electricity-generated estimate.

Artwork is unmodified wiki imagery credited to RimWorld Wiki / Ludeon Studios. [Image source URLs and SHA-256 hashes](assets/production/sources.json) accompany the files. A representative material/design is shown; the fueled-stove image supplied by the wiki is a JPEG. Transparent padding is accounted for separately from collision footprints.
